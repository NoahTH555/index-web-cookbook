One-Pan Garlic Chicken

Easy weeknight chicken baked with garlic, herbs, potatoes, and vegetables in one pan.

Dinner · Ready in about 35–45 minutes · Serves 3–4

Ingredients

  • 600–800 g chicken thighs or breasts
  • 500 g potatoes, cut into small chunks
  • 2–3 carrots, sliced (or other vegetables you like)
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 3–4 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt (or to taste)
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • 1–2 teaspoons dried herbs (for example thyme, oregano, or Italian seasoning)
  • Optional: lemon wedges and fresh parsley for serving
