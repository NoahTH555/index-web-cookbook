Crunchy Chocolate Bars

Ready in 40 min • Serves 8

Crispy wafer-style bars covered in smooth milk chocolate for the perfect “crisp, chocolate vibe”.

Ingredients

  • Wafers or biscuit sticks
  • Milk chocolate
  • Butter or coconut oil

Steps

  1. Melt the chocolate with a little butter or oil.
  2. Dip the wafers until fully coated.
  3. Let them set on baking paper until firm.
Breakfast

Fluffy Pancakes

Ready in 25 min • Serves 4

Light, fluffy pancakes that work with berries, chocolate, or syrup.

Ingredients

  • Flour, baking powder, sugar, salt
  • Milk and eggs
  • Butter for the pan

Steps

  1. Whisk dry ingredients in one bowl, wet in another.
  2. Combine gently and let the batter rest for a few minutes.
  3. Fry small scoops on medium heat until golden on both sides.
Dinner

One-Pan Garlic Chicken

Ready in 35 min • Serves 3–4

Easy weeknight chicken with garlic, herbs, and roasted vegetables.

Ingredients

  • Chicken thighs or breasts
  • Potatoes and vegetables
  • Garlic, herbs, oil, salt, pepper

Steps

  1. Toss chicken and veggies with oil, garlic, and herbs.
  2. Spread on a pan and bake until the chicken is cooked.
  3. Rest a few minutes before serving.
