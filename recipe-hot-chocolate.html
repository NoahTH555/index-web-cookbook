Simple Hot Chocolate

Warm, creamy hot chocolate that is quick to make and perfect with something crispy and chocolatey on the side.

Drink · Ready in about 10 minutes · Serves 2

Ingredients

  • 500 ml milk
  • 2–3 tablespoons cocoa powder
  • 2–3 tablespoons sugar (adjust to taste)
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla sugar or a few drops vanilla extract
  • Optional: a pinch of salt
  • Optional toppings: whipped cream, mini marshmallows, grated chocolate

Steps

  1. Pour the milk into a small pot and heat gently on medium heat.
  2. In a cup or bowl, mix cocoa powder, sugar, and a splash of warm milk into a smooth paste.
  3. Whisk the cocoa paste into the warm milk in the pot until fully mixed.
  4. Add vanilla and a tiny pinch of salt, then keep heating until hot but not boiling.
  5. Pour into mugs and add whipped cream, marshmallows, or grated chocolate on top if you like.

Enjoy immediately, preferably with a crunchy chocolate bar or cookie.
